Focus Shield

The apps that pull you away stay shut while you work.

Pick the apps that pull you away and they stay shut while a session runs. You can switch it off whenever you want, and the list never leaves your phone.

  • It works while reading tooThe same shield is on during a book session, if Instagram keeps calling.
