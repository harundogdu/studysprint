Privacy Policy

How Study Sprint Timer handles your data.

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Overview

Study Sprint Timer ("the app") is an offline-first focus and Pomodoro study timer. We built it to respect your privacy. The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, or any personal details to work.

Information We Collect

We (the developer) do not collect or store your personal data on any server. All of your app data is stored locally on your device only. If you use the Apple Watch app, your data syncs directly between your iPhone and Watch and never reaches us. The one exception is the optional book search described below, which sends the title you type to Open Library.

Advertising & Third-Party Services

The free version of the app displays ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and process certain information to deliver and measure ads. This processing is performed by Google, not by us.

  • We use Google's User Messaging Platform to request your consent where required.
  • On iOS, we show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the app still works fully.
  • Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchasing Premium removes all ads.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment information. To check whether your subscription is active, and to carry your sync code between your own devices, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives the purchase events and an identifier the app generates; it never receives your payment details or your name. See Apple's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Apple Health (Optional)

If you turn on Apple Health sync, the app writes your completed focus sessions to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes. Only the session duration is written; the app reads nothing from Health. Your Health data stays on your device under Apple's Health privacy protections, and you can turn this off anytime in iOS Settings → Health.

Book Search (Optional)

When you add a book to your library, the app can fill in its details for you. As you type a title, that text is sent to Open Library so the app can suggest matching titles, authors and page counts, and cover images are loaded from Open Library's servers. This happens only while you are typing in the search field. We never receive or store these searches, and you can ignore the suggestions and type everything yourself. Without an internet connection the search returns nothing and the rest of the app keeps working. Open Library is run by the Internet Archive; see their privacy policy.

Apple Calendar (Optional)

If you turn on calendar sync, the app writes each completed session into a separate "Study Sprint" calendar in your Calendar app. It only writes entries; it does not read your existing events and does not change your other calendars. Those entries stay in your own calendar account, so we never see them. You can turn the option off in the app's Settings, or revoke access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

Focus Shield (Optional)

Focus Shield keeps the apps you choose closed while a session runs. It uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework, which asks for your permission the first time you turn it on. The apps you pick are stored on your device as tokens only iOS can read, so the list never reaches us. Turn the shield off in the app's Settings, or revoke the permission in iOS Settings → Screen Time.

Game Center (Optional)

If you sign in to Game Center, the app submits two numbers to the leaderboards: your focus minutes for the week and your longest streak. Those go to Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and we never see them. Staying signed out only costs you the leaderboards and achievements; everything else works the same.

The Android Version

On Android, purchases go through Google Play instead of Apple, and the app includes Firebase Analytics, which sends usage measurements to Google. Apple Health, Apple Calendar, the Apple Watch app and Focus Shield are iPhone features and have no Android counterpart. The rest of this policy holds for both versions: your study data stays on your device and we run no server of our own.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not share your locally stored study data with anyone.

Your Choices

  • Manage tracking via iOS Settings.
  • Reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings.
  • Remove ads entirely by purchasing Premium.
  • Delete all local data from the app's Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
